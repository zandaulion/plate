# First-release screenshot plan

Use authentic captures of Bitey running on an Android phone or emulator. Do not
use device frames or marketing claims inside the image. Capture in portrait at
1080 × 1920 or higher, with a clean status bar and no notifications.

The first four images should be the same language as the Play listing:

1. **Your day** — a populated day summary with Bitey, calories, macro split,
   quick bites, and a few logged meals. Alt text: “Bitey's daily food log with
   calories, macros, quick bites, and meals.”
2. **Manual entry** — the manual food editor with an ordinary ingredient and
   nutrition fields. Alt text: “Manual food entry with portion and nutrition
   details.”
3. **Barcode privacy choice** — the consent prompt before an optional Open Food
   Facts lookup. Alt text: “Barcode lookup asks permission before contacting
   Open Food Facts.”
4. **Weight trends** — populated weight and food-trend charts. Alt text:
   “Weight and food trends over time.”
5. **Private backup** — Settings showing backup and restore controls. Alt text:
   “Settings explain that the diary and backup remain under the user's control.”

Google Play requires at least two screenshots. Four portrait screenshots at
1080 × 1920 give the listing eligibility for its recommended app-display
surfaces. Keep any optional overlay copy below 20% of an image and localize it
with the rest of the listing.
